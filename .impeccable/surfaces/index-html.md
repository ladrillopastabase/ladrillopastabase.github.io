# Portfolio home (index.html)

Mode: Experience/Persuade. Audience: industry recruiters and tech visitors, equal weight. Action: open CV (LinkedIn, URL pending) or contact; browse live GitHub repos.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A calm, luminous glass instrument panel for one person: two sides of a profile held in frosted panes over slow light. Refuses the dark neon-glow portfolio with word-blur reveals and tilting cards.
OWN-WORLD: Light cool ground (#eef1f6) with four soft, slowly drifting colour fields (periwinkle, aqua, peach, lilac) seen through frosted white glass: 1px white rim, inner top highlight, low blue-grey shadow. Ink #0b1020 type, one accent electric blue #2f45ff. Funnel Display (headings) / Funnel Sans (text) / Martian Mono (data only); changed from Geist after the detector flagged it as overused. Pill controls. Glass edges lit by a gradient rim.
STORY: Visitor sees who he is and both sides in one viewport, scans industrial practice areas, sees live GitHub work with demos, then opens CV or copies email.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Floating glass nav pill top centre with sliding indicator and CV button. Left 60%: large ink headline, lead, ink primary + glass secondary pill. Right: profile glass pane with photo, name, location, email copy, LinkedIn/GitHub.
FORM: brief-pinned (user: "claro, elegante, glassy futurista"); seed: none (unavailable)
Motion grammar: one ease (0.22,1,0.36,1); hero stagger 90ms/900ms fade-rise 16px; individual panes fade-rise once (never section wrappers: opacity breaks backdrop blur); live GitHub rows arrive staggered 60ms; pointer specular sheen on glass; sliding nav indicator; ambient field drift 40-60s. No tilt, magnet, marquee, blur-words, spinning, counters.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
